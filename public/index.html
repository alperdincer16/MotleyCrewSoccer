How to play

WASD or arrows: move
Space or click: kick (aim with the mouse)
C or Shift: sprint; sprint into the ball carrier to tackle
Hold kick to charge a harder shot
F or right-click: lob over players
Sweep the mouse sideways (or run across the ball) as you kick to curve it
E: your nation's signature move when the meter is full
Three passes in a row: combo shot
1–6: quick shouts (Pass! Shoot! Back! Nice! Sorry! Wow!)
Enter: chat
